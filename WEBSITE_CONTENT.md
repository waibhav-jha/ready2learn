## 7. SCREEN-SMART PEDIATRIC CALCULATOR / EVALUATOR
- **Category Badge**: Interactive Pediatric Calculator
- **Section Heading**: Child Growth & Milestone Evaluator
- **Description**: Select your child's age group to view instant, evidence-based screen time limits and pediatric guidelines crafted by Dr. Chitra Sankar.

### Age Guidelines Breakdown:

#### 1. Infants (0 - 18 Months)
- **Recommended Daily Limit**: Zero Passive Media (Focus on human interaction)
- **Allowed Exception**: Live video calls with family & grandparents are allowed.
- **Core Developmental Goal**: Sensory, motor, and speech development through direct eye contact and play.
- **Pediatric Action Steps**:
  - Prioritize eye contact, singing, talking, and reading physical books.
  - Keep televisions off in the background to prevent speech delay.
  - Focus on tactile toys, floor tummy time, and vocal turn-taking.

#### 2. Toddlers (18 - 24 Months)
- **Recommended Daily Limit**: Max 15 - 30 Mins / Day of educational co-viewing
- **Allowed Exception**: Co-viewing required with an active parent explaining content.
- **Core Developmental Goal**: Language acquisition, motor coordination, and real-world exploration.
- **Pediatric Action Steps**:
  - Choose slow-paced, non-stimulating educational content.
  - Never use screens as a routine soothing mechanism during meals.
  - Maintain strictly screen-free bedrooms and dining areas.

#### 3. Preschoolers (2 - 5 Years)
- **Recommended Daily Limit**: Max 1 Hour / Day of quality programming
- **Allowed Exception**: Turn off all media 1 to 2 hours before bedtime.
- **Core Developmental Goal**: Social-emotional regulation, storytelling, and imaginative play.
- **Pediatric Action Steps**:
  - Avoid fast-paced, high-saturation, or ad-driven videos.
  - Engage in interactive conversation about story concepts.
  - Balance every minute of media with active physical movement.

#### 4. School-Age (6 - 12 Years)
- **Recommended Daily Limit**: 1 to 1.5 Hours / Day (Non-academic media)
- **Allowed Exception**: No devices allowed in bedrooms overnight.
- **Core Developmental Goal**: Balancing hobbies, outdoor sports, sleep, and academic studies.
- **Pediatric Action Steps**:
  - Establish a transparent Family Routine Plan with set device-free windows.
  - Teach basic digital safety, privacy, and respectful communication.
  - Encourage non-digital hobbies, reading, and team sports.

#### 5. Teens (13 - 18 Years)
- **Recommended Daily Limit**: 2 Hours / Day non-academic media
- **Allowed Exception**: Charge devices outside the bedroom every night.
- **Core Developmental Goal**: Mental wellness, self-regulation, and healthy digital balance.
- **Pediatric Action Steps**:
  - Have open, non-judgmental discussions regarding social media pressure.
  - Enforce device-free family dinners and night-time curfews.
  - Encourage independent time management and sleep hygiene.
